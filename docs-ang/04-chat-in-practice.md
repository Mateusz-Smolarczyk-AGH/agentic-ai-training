# Chat in practice — the model's knowledge

[← Previous: Theory — what a chat is](03-chat-theory.md) · [Agenda](00-agenda.md) · [Next: What chat cannot do — no tools →](05-what-chat-cannot-do.md)

`chat` answers **from the model's knowledge** — from what the model learned during training. It does
not check anything in the system or on the network. In this workshop `chat` is the **default agent**
(without tools).

> 🚀 **Paste into opencode:** any general-knowledge question — **make up your own**, e.g.:

```text
Who is currently the president of the USA?
```

```text
How much is 2 + 2 × 7? Show the calculation.
```

See: the answer arrives **at once, from the model's memory**. There was no `bash`, `read` or
`webfetch` — the model did not reach anywhere, it just answered with what it "knows".

## Done when…

- you got an answer to a general-knowledge question,
- you understand that chat answers from the model's memory, without checking the world.
